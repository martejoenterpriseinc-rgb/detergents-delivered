# Expenses and mileage

The Reports workspace now links to database-backed expense and mileage records. Administrators can record expenses (date, category, USD amount, merchant/memo) and their own manual vehicle trips (business date, active vehicle, whole-mile starting/ending odometer, purpose). CPA users have read/export access only. Inventory staff, customers and unauthenticated callers have no access to these records.

Records persist in the existing Expense, ExpenseCategory and MileageTrip tables. No schema migration or existing-record backfill is required. Corrections require an explanation and the current revision. Every successful save writes its audit record in the same transaction, including the prior record for corrections. Stale saves fail instead of overwriting another edit. The interface preserves input after failure; a retained request key prevents an ambiguous/lost response from creating duplicates. Reusing a key for changed input fails.

Mileage is calculated from the two odometer readings; callers cannot supply a separate distance. Vehicle locking prevents simultaneous overlapping odometer ranges. Trips associated with routes or recorded by another person cannot be corrected through this manual-entry workflow, and saved trips cannot be reassigned to another vehicle. Inactive vehicles cannot receive new manual trips. Trip dates represent business calendar days, not exact departure/arrival timestamps. Chicago day boundaries, including daylight-saving transitions, govern mileage filtering.

Both workspaces provide date filters, 50-record pages, totals from the complete selected range and CSV exports up to 10,000 records. Larger exports require a narrower date range; no silently truncated export is returned. USD expense totals exclude other currencies. Unknown mileage remains visibly unknown. Export cells use the existing formula-injection escaping and authenticated, private/no-store responses. The API is GET/POST /api/admin/finance; service-level authorization rechecks the current database identity/roles, with same-origin and bounded JSON checks on writes.

Totals are recorded operating amounts and distance, not profit, deductible expenses or tax-return advice. This increment does not add receipt upload, automatic bank feeds, an accountant-facing correction-history viewer, QuickBooks posting, tax deductions, or changes to orders/payments/stock. Expenses already linked to QuickBooks require reconciliation rather than a local edit. CPA reports, receipt attachments, reconciliation, quarterly subscriptions, provider acceptance, approved catalog/capacity, business setup and launch activation remain separate unfinished launch work.

Validation includes amount/date/odometer rules, DST boundaries, native PostgreSQL duplicate/concurrent saves, overlapping trips, audit rollback and role boundaries, plus desktop/tablet/mobile entry/reload/correction/export, lost-response retry and CPA read-only checks. Hosted acceptance is recorded in the release PR after required CI succeeds.
