# Reviewed QuickBooks expense posting

The QuickBooks screen lists saved expenses in 50-row pages. An administrator prepares a draft using the saved category mapping and an explicit bank/cash or credit-card payment type, reviews the source amount/date/memo and mapped company/accounts, and confirms submission. CPA access remains read-only. Expense posting is separate from sending money: the Purchase API records the existing expense in the books; it does not initiate a bank payment.

`QboExpenseExport` retains immutable source, account mapping, payload and generated document reference. A partial unique index permits only one non-canceled export for an expense. Preparing a draft freezes the expense's accounting fields. An unsubmitted draft can be canceled to permit correction/re-preparation; submitted or uncertain exports cannot be canceled or replaced. Database triggers preserve export identity, evidence and forward-only state changes, and prevent rewriting established expense accounting links.

The provider POST is attempted at most once per export after a durable, audited claim. Concurrent calls and retries see the existing claim and cannot submit another transaction. Both mapped accounts are re-read and checked; changed mappings, company/connection, source, roles, account state/type/currency or failed audit stop submission. The provider adapter performs no automatic POST retries.

A timeout, crash or unsuccessful confirmation leaves the export submitted/unknown and its source locked. Reconciliation performs a read-only lookup by its generated document number. Exactly one matching Purchase must agree on date, payment type, USD currency, accounts, description, line/total cents and zero additional tax before the external ID, source link and audit commit atomically. No match, duplicates or conflicting evidence require accounting review. Reconciliation never creates a replacement transaction. A claim interrupted before the network call also remains blocked: first establish what exists in the intended company; do not clear the claim or blindly repeat the POST.

Current expense records have a single saved gross amount and no tax-component split. This exporter does not invent a tax allocation, reclaim purchase tax or reclassify existing amounts. It supports one account-based expense line, with bank/cash or credit-card payment types. Checks, multi-currency expenses, bills/payables, sales/refunds and COGS posting require their own reviewed workflows. Legacy accounting-linked expenses are not reposted. New links use `qbo:<mode>:<realm>:<id>` and the separate provider ID is retained in export evidence.

Submission defaults off. Enable only after intended-company sandbox acceptance and posting review using `DD_QBO_EXPENSE_POSTING_ENABLED=true` and `DD_QBO_EXPENSE_POSTING_COMPANY=<sandbox|live>:<realm>`. These settings authorize the adapter; they are not acceptance evidence. Neither setting was enabled on a hosted service during this build. Preparation and read-only reconciliation are independently available with an authorized connection.

Migration `20260916170000_quickbooks_expense_exports` is additive and unperformed on hosted databases. The existing release checkpoint and retained migration-history review remain required. All automated test records and Intuit responses are synthetic/mocked. Browser tests simulate a lost submit response followed by reconciliation; native tests establish source locking, concurrency, immutable records and audit rollback. No real company transaction or public launch is claimed.

Primary implementation references: [Intuit Purchase model](https://github.com/intuit/QuickBooks-V3-PHP-SDK/blob/master/src/Data/IPPPurchase.php) (required PaymentType, payment account, output-only total) and [Intuit DataService](https://github.com/intuit/QuickBooks-V3-PHP-SDK/blob/master/src/DataService/DataService.php) (create and company-scoped query operations). No provider request-ID retention guarantee is assumed; duplicate prevention rests on the durable one-submit claim and read-only reconciliation.
