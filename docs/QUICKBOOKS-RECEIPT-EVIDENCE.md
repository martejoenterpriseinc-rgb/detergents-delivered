# Cash receipt compiler and evidence matching

This increment implements the pure receipt compiler and receipt evidence matcher. It does not add a posting endpoint, enable a provider call, or claim that SalesReceipt/RefundReceipt acceptance is complete.

The compiler accepts original-record sale evidence from `sales-refund-source.ts`, an optional matched settlement, and company-verified customer/item/clearing-account mappings. Each sale line uses its original net amount after promotion and reward deductions. Quantity and original unit price remain in the source evidence; they are deliberately not used to divide a discounted net line into rounded provider unit prices. Reward restoration is never added to a cash refund receipt. Reward-only and compensation entries are rejected by this cash compiler. Unmatched refund tax is rejected even when the tax amount is zero.

Tax treatment is supplied explicitly as the US TAX/NON classification for each linked item. Zero tax does not imply NON. A positive original line tax cannot be changed to NON. The original destination and total tax are copied; no current catalog price or reconstructed tax rate is consulted. Sale/refund identifiers have distinct document prefixes. The compiler checks identities, unique lines, quantities, integer-cent totals, date validity and original source limits before generating a payload.

The evidence matcher requires the correct entity, document, business date, USD cash total, customer, clearing account, destination, original net lines and tax total. Changed tax, extra discounts, grouped lines, duplicate items, altered item links, currency conversion and outstanding balances fail verification. A single exact computed subtotal may follow the business lines. When provider tax detail lines are supplied, their total must reconcile too. A matching total does not by itself verify tax-agency allocation or provider acceptance.

Before this compiler can be connected to posting, the application still needs durable receipt drafts/claims, immutable source and mapping storage, original posted-sale linkage for refunds, explicit company-bound submission controls, read-only recovery after uncertain writes, and operator monitoring. Company tax preferences and tax-agency treatment require acceptance with the intended US QuickBooks company. Confirm the refund clearing-account behavior in that acceptance; SDK base-class field annotations alone are not sufficient proof. Do not route compensations through a new positive sales receipt or mark their tax verified.

Primary schema references inspected: Intuit's [SalesTransaction](https://github.com/intuit/QuickBooks-V3-PHP-SDK/blob/master/src/Data/IPPSalesTransaction.php), [SalesReceipt](https://github.com/intuit/QuickBooks-V3-PHP-SDK/blob/master/src/Data/IPPSalesReceipt.php), and [RefundReceipt](https://github.com/intuit/QuickBooks-V3-PHP-SDK/blob/master/src/Data/IPPRefundReceipt.php). These describe schema fields; synthetic tests and schema inspection do not establish real provider acceptance.
